Fig. 2. Joint evaluation of threshold strictness and burst length $L \in \{1,3,5\}$ across 20 evaluation episodes. The source dataset contains $N=26{,}000$ raw packet transmissions across 60 devices (200--500 packets per device); $N$ is the dataset total, not the cumulative episode evaluation count. (a) Mean operating points; the strict threshold $\tau_{\text{robust}}$ lowers $\text{FAR}_B$ from 11.9% to 4.4% at the cost of raising $\text{FRR}$ from 8.5% to 30.1%. Fusing $L=5$ packets moves both operating points toward lower $\text{FRR}$: $\text{FRR}$ 8.5% $\rightarrow$ 3.5% at $\tau_{\text{Youden}}$ ($\text{FAR}_B$ 11.9% $\rightarrow$ 10.5%) and 30.1% $\rightarrow$ 18.1% at $\tau_{\text{robust}}$ ($\text{FAR}_B$ 4.4% $\rightarrow$ 6.4%). (b) $\text{FAR}_B$ (solid) and $\text{FRR}$ (dashed) versus $L$. Bands show $\pm 1$ standard error over 20 episodes. Fusion was evaluated at both thresholds on the same episodes and seeds. Three episodes lie outside panel (a)'s x-range, including Ep 4 ($\text{FRR}$ 84.6%, $\text{FAR}_B$ 4.3%).
